Privacy Policy for Xplain NURSING

Xplain NURSING ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Xplain NURSING mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app and the choices you have associated with that data.

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you:

Use of Data

Xplain Nursing uses the collected data for various purposes:

Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. We utilize secure backend services (Supabase) with encryption protocols, though remember that no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.
